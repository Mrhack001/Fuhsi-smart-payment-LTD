Common Questions

Got Questions?
We Have Answers.

What is PayWithMeritIQ? ▼

PayWithMeritIQ is an intelligent payment support platform built under the FUHSI MeritIQ ecosystem. It exists to help FUHSI students and newly admitted aspirants navigate the fee payment process with confidence. Rather than struggling to identify the right fees, amounts, and payment channels on your own, PayWithMeritIQ guides you through the entire process — from understanding your fee category to completing the payment through the correct official FUHSI channel, all with dedicated human support along the way.

Is PayWithMeritIQ an official FUHSI payment platform? ▼

PayWithMeritIQ is a guided payment support service built under FUHSI MeritIQ — a student-focused intelligence platform. It is not directly operated by FUHSI's Bursary or Finance Division. However, all payments facilitated through PayWithMeritIQ are directed exclusively to official FUHSI payment channels. We act as a support layer that helps you pay correctly, not as a replacement for FUHSI's official processes. Always confirm final payment receipts with the university's Bursary office.

Why is there a service charge? ▼

The service charge exists to cover the personalised support PayWithMeritIQ provides for every student. This includes: verifying your correct fee amount, providing step-by-step payment guidance, communicating with you via WhatsApp or phone, coordinating your transaction with the right official channels, and following up until you have your confirmed receipt. This is NOT an extra university fee — it is a small charge for the value of having a dedicated team handle the payment process on your behalf, so you avoid costly mistakes, wrong payments, or being scammed. You are informed of the exact service charge before you proceed.

What fees can I pay through PayWithMeritIQ? ▼

PayWithMeritIQ supports guidance for all major FUHSI fee categories, including: Acceptance Fee (for newly admitted students), School Fees (session tuition), Hostel Fee (optional on-campus accommodation), Departmental Charges (department-specific operational fees), Laboratory Charges (for science and health programmes), and Clinical Training Charges (for MBBS, Nursing, Physiotherapy, and other clinical courses). If you have a specific charge not listed here, contact our support team and we will verify and guide you accordingly.

How do I know the exact amount I should pay? ▼

Use the "Check Fee Structure" tool on this page to view estimated fee amounts per course. These figures are based on available information and are clearly labelled as estimates. For the definitive, official amount, always verify directly with FUHSI's Bursar's Office before making any payment. Once you submit a payment request through PayWithMeritIQ, our team also cross-checks your specific fee details before guiding you to make the transfer — so you always pay the right amount.

How long does payment processing take? ▼

After you submit your payment request, our team typically reaches out within a few hours during business hours. Once you complete the payment through the guided process, confirmation and receipt delivery usually happens within the same business day. Processing times may vary depending on bank transfer timelines and FUHSI's Bursary verification schedule. We keep you informed at every step via WhatsApp or your provided contact details.

Will I receive payment confirmation and a receipt? ▼

Yes. After successful payment, you will receive a payment confirmation and a receipt via WhatsApp or email. This receipt includes your student details, fee type, amount paid, transaction ID, timestamp, and verified status. For academic purposes, the authoritative receipt is issued by FUHSI's Bursary — our team assists you in obtaining and verifying that official receipt as part of the guided process.

Can I contact support before paying? ▼

Absolutely. You are encouraged to contact our support team before initiating any payment if you have questions or need clarity. You can reach us via WhatsApp, phone call, or email — all contact details are available after submitting a payment request, or you can request them directly. Our team is available to answer pre-payment questions, explain the service charge, and help you decide whether PayWithMeritIQ is right for your situation.

What happens if my payment fails or is not confirmed? ▼

If a payment fails or is unconfirmed, contact our support team immediately — do NOT attempt to re-pay without guidance, as this can lead to duplicate transactions. Our team will investigate the issue, check with the relevant payment channel, and advise you on next steps. We coordinate directly with FUHSI's Bursary where necessary to resolve discrepancies. Your money and your record are protected throughout this process.

Is my personal information secure? ▼

Yes. All personal information you provide — including your name, matric number, phone number, and payment details — is handled with strict confidentiality. Your data is used only to process your payment request and communicate with you. It is never shared with third parties outside of the payment process. Communications are conducted through secure channels, and our team is bound by a privacy-first policy. If you have specific concerns about data security, contact us before submitting your request.
